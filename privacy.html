Privacy Policy (English summary)

Starshopping is an online retailer based in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia. We take orders through our Facebook Page, Messenger chat, and website.

What we collect: your name, phone number, delivery address, the content of your Messenger conversation with us, your order history, and the Messenger page-scoped user ID (PSID) that Facebook supplies. We never collect passwords, payment card numbers, or national ID numbers.

Why: to record, confirm and deliver your order, to contact you about it, to handle returns and complaints, and to understand which products are in demand.

Automation: our Messenger page uses an automated assistant that reads your messages to answer product questions and record orders. You can ask for a human at any time.

Storage: data is held in a password-protected PostgreSQL database (Supabase), accessible only to authorised Starshopping staff.

Sharing: we do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising. We share name, phone and address with our delivery partner only as needed to deliver your order, and with authorities where Mongolian law requires it.

Retention: order records for 2 years; chat conversations for 12 months; sooner if you ask us to delete them.

Your rights and data deletion: you may ask what we hold, correct it, or have it deleted. Email ariunbold.agency@gmail.com with the phone number used for your order, or message our Messenger page asking for deletion. We respond within 30 days.

Children: our service is not directed at anyone under 18.
